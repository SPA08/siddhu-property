Agricultural Trading Hub

Grain Market (Galla Mandi)

One of the largest grain markets in Sahiwal division, established during the British canal colony expansion. Major trading point for wheat, cotton, corn, and oilseeds.

  • Primary Trade: Wheat, Cotton, Corn, Fertilizer
  • Property Type: Commercial shops, commission shops, godowns
  • Location: Railway Station Road Corridor
Retail & Apparel Center

Rail Bazaar & Saddar Market

The traditional 12-bazaar planned grid center of Chichawatni city, housing clothing boutiques, footwear, gold markets, and household goods.

  • Layout: Historical Grid Bazaars (1 to 12)
  • Products: Textiles, Jewelry, Footwear, Crockery
  • Footfall: High daily consumer density
Modern Commercial Strip

Tower Road Shopping Strip

Modern commercial sector featuring banks (HBL, UBL, Meezan), pharmaceutical centers, corporate offices, and property advisory firms in Hayat Abad.

  • Key Anchor: Siddhu Property Advisor (Street 08)
  • Tenants: Commercial Banks, Clinics, Agencies
  • Access: Direct link to GT Road & Hayat Abad
