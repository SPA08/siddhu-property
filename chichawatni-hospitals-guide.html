Hospitals & Healthcare in Chichawatni

Verified guide to primary hospitals, government health facilities, private medical complexes, and emergency response access across Chichawatni.

Official Government Facility

THQ Hospital Chichawatni

Tehsil Headquarter Hospital is the flagship government medical institution in Chichawatni, offering 24/7 emergency care, OPD, surgery, pediatric, and maternity services.

  • Emergency: 24/7 Response Center
  • Location: Civil Lines Area, Chichawatni
  • Nearby Colonies: Civil Lines, Model Town, Hayat Abad
Public Health Center

Civil Hospital & Dispensaries

Government dispensaries and primary outpatient facilities serving urban residential sectors with basic healthcare, vaccination, and diagnostic labs.

  • Services: OPD, Vaccination, Basic Diagnostic
  • Location: Station Road / Municipal Sector
  • Nearby Colonies: Housing Colony, Block 1-4
Private Healthcare

Private Clinics & Medical Complexes

Private specialist clinics, eye care centers, and diagnostic laboratories concentrated along Tower Road and GT Road access points.

  • Specialties: Cardiology, Gynaecology, Ophthalmology
  • Location: Tower Road Commercial Strip
  • Nearby Colonies: Hayat Abad, Ghaffor Town
