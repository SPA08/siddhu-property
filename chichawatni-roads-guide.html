Infrastructure Directory

Main Roads & Arteries in Chichawatni

Comprehensive guide to primary transport corridors, street width standards, traffic accessibility, and residential/commercial connectivity across Chichawatni.

N-5 National Highway

GT Road Corridor

Primary national highway connecting Chichawatni eastwards to Sahiwal/Lahore and westwards to Multan. Prime location for commercial showrooms, plazas, and petrol pumps.

  • Width: 110 ft dual carriageway
  • Key Junctions: Bypass Chowk, Railway Crossing, Kamalia Turn
  • Connected Colonies: Housing Scheme, Chak 39/12-L
Commercial Artery

Tower Road

The central commercial spine of Hayat Abad (Chak 40/12-L), housing Siddhu Property Advisor Office (Street 08), commercial banks, retail stores, and medical clinics.

  • Main Width: 40 ft - 50 ft
  • Branch Streets: 20 ft - 30 ft residential galis
  • Connected Colonies: Hayat Abad, Ghaffor Town, Gulberg
Educational & Residential Hub

College Road

Key arterial link passing by Govt Degree College for Boys and leading educational institutes. High demand for student housing, family residences, and coaching centers.

  • Main Width: 40 ft
  • Character: High footfall, educational & residential
  • Connected Colonies: Model Town, Civil Lines
Historical Grain Market Corridor

Railway & Grain Market Road

Connects Chichawatni Railway Station to the historic Galla Mandi (Grain Market) and Rail Bazaar. Major hub for agricultural trading and wholesale markets.

  • Main Width: 35 ft - 45 ft
  • Character: High commercial density, wholesale trading
  • Connected Bazaars: Rail Bazaar, Saddar Market
